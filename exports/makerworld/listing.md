# MakerWorld listing: tea bag drawer divider

## Title

Spring-Loaded Drawer Divider for Tea Bags (Customizable Width, No Glue)

## Category

Household > Kitchen

## Tags

drawer divider, tea, tea bags, kitchen organizer, drawer organizer, tension fit, compliant mechanism, customizable, parametric, no glue

## Description

A thin divider that stands across a kitchen drawer to keep tea bags sorted. It holds itself in place: a flexible spring at each end pushes small pads against the drawer's side walls. No glue, no screws, no tape. It pops out when you want to move it.

**How it works**
- The wall is one flat 1.2 mm sheet. Each end has a spring made of thin slots cut into the sheet, so it squeezes shorter along its length.
- Two small T-shaped pads clip onto the ends and press flat against the drawer sides.
- It's built 6 mm longer than the drawer, so each spring squeezes in 3 mm once it's in.
- The middle is open below a 25 mm top strip, which keeps it light (about 15 g in total).

**Fit your drawer**
Click **Customize** and enter your drawer's inside width (measure where the divider will sit, to within about 1 mm). The springs absorb about 1.5 mm of error in total.

Size limits: the wall prints flat, corner to corner on the plate, so the plate sets the longest drawer:
- 256 mm plates (A1, P1, X1, X2D): drawers 150 to 295.5 mm wide
- A1 mini: up to 188 mm

Wider drawers won't fit in one piece.

The ready-made profile is for a **290 mm** drawer.

**Printing**
- PLA. The profile uses Bambu PLA Matte.
- 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, 3 walls, 25% gyroid infill.
- No supports. The wall prints flat on the diagonal, and the pads print face-down.
- About 23 minutes on an X2D.

**Assembly**
1. Slide a T-pad down onto the post at each end of the wall. The ends of the clip are a little looser so it starts easily, then it grips.
2. Squeeze the divider slightly and lower it into the drawer.

**Tips**
- If it feels loose, measure the drawer again at the spot where the divider sits. Drawers are often narrower or wider in the middle.
- If the spring slots close up completely, the divider is too long for that spot. Re-customize with the real width.
- Anything shorter than about 35 mm, like a tea bag lying flat, can slide under the middle.
