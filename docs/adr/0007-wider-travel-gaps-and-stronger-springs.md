# Wider Travel gaps and stronger Springs (v2)

In the v1 test the Travel gaps closed (Bottoming out) and the Springs felt weak. How far a Spring closes once installed is set by geometry, not by its strength, so the two were fixed separately. The Travel gaps went from 5 to 7 mm, giving 3.25 mm of headroom beyond the designed 3.75 mm maximum. The Beams went from 0.8 to 0.95 mm thick, making the Springs about 1.7x stronger: about 1.8 N per end at the nominal Compression (1.3-2.2 N across the Drawer span tolerance), with a peak Beam stress of 16.2 MPa. That remains low enough for PLA (ADR 0004).

## Consequences

The cause of the v1 Bottoming out was never pinned down. Likely candidates are the drawer being narrower than 290 mm where the Divider sits, or the Divider being pushed during use. If v2 bottoms out too, measure the Drawer span at that spot before changing the Springs.
