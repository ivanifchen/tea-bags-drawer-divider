# Springs at the ends, not spread along the Wall

The Springs push along the whole Wall, so the Wall is always under compression. A Wall that is springy along its full length has to be soft in bending, and a soft Wall under compression bows. Holding about 1.5 N straight over 290 mm while compliant everywhere would take a Wall about 50 mm thick. So the Body stays as stiff as the thickness limit allows, and all the compliance sits in a short Spring at each end.

## Considered Options

- **Uniform slatted Wall (rails + slats the whole length, springs formed by breaks in the rails):** looked clean, but needed 4 mm deep rails; at the 1.5 mm limit (ADR 0003) open slats leave the Body about 7x floppier than a solid sheet.
- **Distributed spring Wall (corrugated or springy everywhere):** bows under its own Compression, which is the failure the user ruled out at the start.
