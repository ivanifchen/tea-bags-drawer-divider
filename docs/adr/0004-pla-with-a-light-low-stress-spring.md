# PLA, with a light, low-stress Spring

The user chose PLA over the recommended PETG. PLA creeps under constant load, so a Spring that stays compressed in a drawer slowly loses force. The Spring is therefore designed light (about 1 N per end at 3 mm of Compression) and with low Beam stress (about 11 MPa nominal, 14 MPa at the narrowest point of the drawer), which slows the creep. The light force also suits the thin Wall, which would bow under a stiffer Spring (ADR 0003). The Free length is sized for this one drawer (290 ± 1.5 mm, 296 mm free); the Springs only absorb that tolerance and print error, and are not meant to fit other drawers.

## Consequences

Expect the grip to soften over months. If it gets too loose, reprint in PETG with the same geometry; PETG has a lower stiffness, so the Spring force will drop and may need retuning.
