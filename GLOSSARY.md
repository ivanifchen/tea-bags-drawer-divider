# Tea bag drawer divider

A printed wall that stands across a kitchen drawer to separate tea, held in place only by spring
tension against the drawer's side walls.

## The divider

**Divider**:
The whole assembly: one Wall plus two T-pads.
_Avoid_: separator, partition

**Wall**:
The long printed part that spans the drawer, including its Body and both Springs.
_Avoid_: plate, sheet, panel

**Body**:
The rigid middle of the Wall, between the two Springs; just a Top strip, open below.
_Avoid_: web, center section, frame

**Top strip**:
The horizontal strip that forms the Body. It carries the Springs' push and keeps the Divider from Bowing.
_Avoid_: top rail, top border, bridge

**Spring**:
The compliant zone at each end of the Wall that lets the Divider shorten along its length and nothing else.
_Avoid_: flexure, tensioner

**Beam**:
One of the thin vertical members inside a Spring; the parts that actually bend.
_Avoid_: slat, finger, rib

**Top bar**:
The strip along the top of a Spring that joins the Body to the tops of the Beams.

**Bottom bar**:
The strip along the bottom of a Spring that joins the feet of the Beams to the Upright.

**Upright**:
The full-height post at each end of the Wall that a T-pad clips onto.
_Avoid_: stem, post

**T-pad**:
The separate small part that clips onto an Upright and presses flat against the drawer's side wall.
_Avoid_: T, foot, end cap, bumper

**Travel gap**:
A clearance slot in a Spring that closes up as the Spring compresses.

**Bottoming out**:
A Spring compressed until a Travel gap closes, after which the end stops behaving like a spring.
_Avoid_: gaps touching, maxed out

## Fit

**Drawer span**:
The measured inside width of the drawer between the two side walls the Divider presses on.
_Avoid_: drawer width

**Free length**:
The Divider's length from T-pad face to T-pad face when nothing is squeezing it.
_Avoid_: total length, nominal length

**Compression**:
How far each Spring is pushed in once the Divider is installed; Free length minus Drawer span, split between the two ends.
_Avoid_: preload, squeeze, tension

**Bowing**:
The Wall curving sideways because the Springs' push along its length exceeds what its thickness can hold straight.
_Avoid_: buckling (fine in analysis, but Bowing is the word for what you see)
